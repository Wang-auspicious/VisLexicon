# Images in the mask studies

These are photographic source assets, not screenshots of another product or generated placeholders. They are served locally so the demonstrations do not depend on a remote image host.

## Fronalpstock

- **Author:** Hannes Röst.
- **Original work:** [Fronalpstock big.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fronalpstock_big.jpg), panorama photographed on 31 July 2010, uploaded 26 August 2010.
- **License selected:** [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
- **Downloaded rendition:** [Wikimedia 1280 × 575 JPEG](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/3/3f/Fronalpstock_big.jpg/1280px-Fronalpstock_big.jpg).
- **Local file:** `fronalpstock.jpg`, retained byte for byte as downloaded. Wikimedia produced the thumbnail from the larger original; no local pixel edit was made.
- **Display changes:** individual demonstrations apply browser crops, clipping, transparency, grayscale, pixel sampling, or tile placement. Any adapted photographic rendition in these demonstrations remains available under CC BY-SA 3.0. The source, author, and license are linked from each study that uses it.
- **Observed content:** clouds, a mountain ridge, green slopes, foreground buildings and trees. The photo is used as a controlled common subject so different masking methods can be compared without changing the subject.
